SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") forms part of the Hosting Services Agreement between Nimbus Cloud Hosting Pvt Ltd ("Provider") and Copperleaf Retail Private Limited ("Customer").

Effective Date: 1 April 2026
SLA Review Date: 30 September 2026

1. AVAILABILITY
1.1 The Provider shall ensure that the hosting platform is available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance.
1.2 The Provider shall give the Customer at least 72 hours' notice of any scheduled maintenance.

2. INCIDENT RESPONSE
2.1 For Priority 1 incidents, the Provider must respond within 30 minutes and restore service within 4 hours.
2.2 For Priority 2 incidents, the Provider must respond within 2 hours.
2.3 The Customer shall report incidents through the support portal or the 24x7 helpline.

3. SERVICE CREDITS
3.1 If monthly availability falls below 99.5%, the Provider shall credit 5% of the monthly hosting fee for each full 0.5% shortfall, up to a maximum of 25% of the monthly hosting fee.
3.2 The Customer must claim service credits within 30 days of the end of the affected month.

4. REPORTING
4.1 The Provider shall publish an availability report to the Customer within 7 days after the end of each month.
